# Project Governance

## Classification

Small tasks stay in the main thread when they are clear, low-risk, and isolated. Medium tasks use a lightweight spec and at most two parallel departments. Large tasks use the complete OpenSpec lifecycle and department coordination.

## Autonomy

Proceed without asking when a safe, conventional choice is enough. Ask only for credentials, irreversible changes, production operations, paid actions, public publishing, important deletion, or product choices with materially different outcomes.

## Source Of Truth

For medium and large work, keep decisions in OpenSpec change files. In fallback mode, keep them under `.project-autopilot/changes/<change-id>/`. Do not scatter authoritative state across chat notes, scratch files, and separate registries.

## Reporting

Final reports should be short and evidence-based: completion result, main changes, checks run, blocked checks, and real residual risk.
